# Maeve Shopping List

## P1S external camera upgrade

Status: Planned - not yet purchased

- [ ] Raspberry Pi 4 Model B, 4 GB or greater
- [ ] Raspberry Pi 4 USB-C power supply, 5 V / 3 A
- [ ] 32 GB or larger high-endurance microSD card
- [ ] 2 x Logitech C920 USB webcams
- [ ] Up to 2 USB extension cables if required by the final camera positions
- [ ] Design and print 2 adjustable external P1S camera enclosures and mounts:
  - low side view for edge lift and warp detection
  - higher front/top view for first-layer coverage, spaghetti, and overall shape

Purpose: provide Maeve and OctoEverywhere with smoother complementary external
P1S views than the factory camera. Target 15-30 FPS per camera while keeping
both cameras outside the heated enclosure. Verify Raspberry Pi USB bandwidth,
power capacity, and full printer travel before selecting final mount positions.

The disabled software foundation is prepared for a private local HTTP/HTTPS
camera source. It rejects public addresses and credential-bearing URLs, and it
supports only conservative read-only results: `looks_good`, `possible_problem`,
or `unable_to_verify`. First-layer, warp, spaghetti, and separation inspection
remain inactive until the hardware is purchased and real labeled prints are
available for validation.

Preserve the factory P1S camera; the external camera supplements it rather than
replacing it. No automatic pause, cancel, or other printer control is authorized.
